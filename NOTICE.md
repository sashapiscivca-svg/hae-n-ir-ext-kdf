## 1. Training and evaluation datasets

The model weights were trained on the datasets below. This repository contains **no images** from them except two HIT-UAV test frames used as test and example inputs (`examples/frames/`, CC0 1.0), and **no annotations**. The splits and conversions are those of the hae project; see the [hae DATA_CARD](https://github.com/sashapiscivca-svg/hae/blob/main/DATA_CARD.md). All five datasets are civilian and allow commercial use and derivative works; the two newer ones are university datasets (Johannes Kepler University Linz, funded by the Austrian Science Fund and the State of Upper Austria; George Mason University, funded by its Geography and Geoinformation Science department).

## 2. Pretrained weights

None from third parties. The model was trained from random initialisation. It was trained with knowledge distillation from a teacher that the hae authors trained themselves from random initialisation on the same five datasets (hae-s-ir-ext, not released). No ImageNet or other third-party checkpoints, teachers or backbones were used.

## 3. Software

The model runs with the [hae](https://github.com/sashapiscivca-svg/hae) package (MIT) or any ONNX runtime. The design draws on published ideas listed in the [hae NOTICE](https://github.com/sashapiscivca-svg/hae/blob/main/NOTICE.md#3-published-ideas-the-design-draws-on); its runtime dependencies (numpy, onnxruntime, onnx, opencv-python-headless, pillow, optionally huggingface_hub) are installed by the user under their own licences.
